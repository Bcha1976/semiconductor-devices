Problem A.1 Two zeros B · Guided calculation

From the course notes, Annex A

A 0.5 kg ball rolls off a table 0.8 m high. (i) Give its potential energy on the table and on the floor, first with the zero at the floor, then with the zero at the table top. (ii) Compute the work done by the weight and the speed on reaching the floor in both cases. What does not depend on the zero?

Show the short answer

(i) Zero at the floor: U=0.5×9.8×0.8=3.92U=0.5\times9.8\times0.8=3.92U=0.5×9.8×0.8=3.92 J on the table, 0 on the floor. Zero at the table: 0 on the table, −3.92-3.92−3.92 J on the floor. (ii) In both cases the weight does W=−ΔU=3.92W=-\Delta U=3.92W=−ΔU=3.92 J and v=2×3.92/0.5=3.96v=\sqrt{2\times3.92/0.5}=3.96v=2×3.92/0.5​=3.96 m/s (for a ball leaving the table at negligible speed). The work, the change of UUU and the speed do not depend on the zero.

Problem A.6 Energy budget of illuminated silicon B · Guided calculation
