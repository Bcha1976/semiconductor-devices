Interactives

Seven animated pieces for the core of the course, Chapters 4 to 8. They are not the figures of the notes: they show the mechanisms moving. Everything is computed from the equations of the course, with its reference junction and transistor; each one also sits in its chapter, at the section it illustrates.

  1. Chapter 5The PN junction forms: a thought experimentBring a P piece and an N piece into contact and watch the junction build itself: carriers diffuse, ions are uncovered, the field grows and drift grows with it until it balances diffusion.
  2. Chapter 5The barrier reading: Boltzmann tails, diffusion and driftThe majority carriers spread in energy (Boltzmann): only those above the step q(Vd − V) can climb it (diffusion), while the minority carriers roll down whatever its height (drift).
  3. Chapter 8The NPN transistor: VBE sets the slope, VCB does nothing until…Control VBE and VCB and see, at the same time, the electron line in the base, the hole bump in the collector, and the point on the IC against VCB characteristic with its plateaus..
  4. Chapter 6Injection under bias and where the current goesBias the long reference diode from reverse to forward.
  5. Chapter 8The Early effect: the moving zero and the common −VARaise VCE: the BC SCR eats into the base, the zero of the base line moves toward the emitter, the slope and IC rise.
  6. Chapter 4Carriers out of equilibrium: light on, light offLight falls on the surface of an N-type sample.
  7. Chapter 7The junction stores charge: CT and CDMove the bias and add a small signal.
