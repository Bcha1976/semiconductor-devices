Problem B.6 A warmer rod B · Guided calculation

From the course notes, Annex B

At 350 K, nin_ini​ is 27.8 times larger than at 300 K and both mobilities are 1.26 times smaller (Chapter 3, Problem 3.15). At the same voltage, by what factor do the current and the dissipated power change? What happens to ℓ\ellℓ? Why can this lead to a runaway if the rod is poorly cooled?

Show the short answer

The conductivity is multiplied by 27.8/1.26=2227.8/1.26=2227.8/1.26=22, and so are the current and the power V2/RV^2/RV2/R. ℓ=kT/qE\ell=kT/q\mathcal{E}ℓ=kT/qE grows in proportion to TTT, by 17 %. More power heats the rod, which raises nin_ini​, hence the current and the power again: without enough cooling the temperature runs away (thermal runaway).
