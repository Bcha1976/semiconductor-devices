INTERACTIVE · CHAPTER 6

Injection under bias and where the current goes

Bias the long reference diode from reverse to forward. The barrier changes by qV, the quasi-Fermi levels split, the minority densities at the edges follow the law of the junction, and the current changes hands along the device.

Chapter 6, Sections 6.12, 6.6, 6.7, 6.10

What to watch
  • Forward bias: the edge densities are multiplied by eV/VT.
  • Reverse bias: the edges empty and the current saturates at −I₀.
  • Along the device the electron and hole parts change, the total current does not.
